# Tag Team Tracker

An unofficial companion app for tracking players, matches, and statistics for the board game **Tag Team**.

Made with AI Studio.

No profit is made from this app.

This is an unofficial, fan-made project. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed, sponsored, or approved by Scorpion Masqué, Gricha German, or Corentin Lebrat**.

All rights to **Tag Team** — including its name, fighters, artwork, and card content — are reserved to **Scorpion Masqué**, and to designers **Gricha German** and **Corentin Lebrat**.

## Data & privacy

All player, match, and statistics data is stored locally on your device / browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server, and no data is shared with third parties.

## Accuracy note

Fighter stats (starting HP and power) were entered manually from the physical fighter boards and may contain small errors. Always double-check against your own components if something looks off — values can be corrected directly in the app.
